Track 02 · projects

“When something is actually truly functional and it’s easy to get to, I love that.”

— Donald Glover

  1. 01

    Sleep Stage Analysis with Deep Learning & XAI

    CNN · LSTM · Transformer · feat. SHAP & LIME

    Final-year research project on automated sleep stage classification from EEG/PSG data. Implemented CNN, LSTM, DNN and Transformer-based architectures, with SHAP and LIME for explainability. Knowledge graphs add interpretability and reasoning over sleep patterns.

  2. 02

    V.A.R.Y.S: Video Analysis for Real-time Yield of Sentiments

    Transformers · weak supervision · multimodal

    A multimodal AI system for context-aware, real-time analysis of social media comment sections. It combines video, audio, metadata and comments to derive stance, sentiment depth, demographic patterns and statistical summaries, going beyond binary sentiment.

    Applied to political unrest, media controversies and social movements, it shows how content, creator influence and context shape opinion at scale.

  3. 03

    Cotton Plant Leaf Disease Detection

    CNN · augmentation · computer vision

    Deep learning models that detect and classify diseases in cotton plant leaves. The focus was preprocessing, data augmentation, CNN architectures and evaluation for real agricultural use.

  4. 04

    Taxi Trip Duration Prediction

    feature engineering · regression

    An ML model predicting taxi trip durations from structured trip data, with feature engineering to measure how temporal, geographic and demand factors affect travel time.

  5. 05

    Air Piano: Gesture-Based Instrument

    Arduino Uno · flex sensors · HCI

    A wearable glove with three flex sensors on an Arduino Uno. Each sensor has two bend thresholds, so one finger plays two tones. Sensor readings are processed in real time and sent to software for audio playback.

    Beyond music, it explores accessibility: simple hand gestures that let elderly or physically impaired users trigger audio cues or alerts.
